## A portfolio that grows with you

Open Studio started with a familiar frustration: portfolios can look great on day one and become difficult to maintain by day thirty. This concept explores a more flexible, content-first alternative.

## Built to be edited

- Projects and posts live in their own Markdown files.
- A small JSON index controls the order and supporting details.
- The site works without a framework or build step.

That structure makes publishing feel more like writing and less like updating a codebase.
