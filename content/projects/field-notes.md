## The idea

Field Notes is a small concept for people who are always collecting interesting things: a sentence from a book, a color on a walk, an idea that arrives on the way home. The goal was to make capturing those moments feel effortless.

## What I worked on

- Mapped the experience from first note to a useful collection.
- Designed a calm, responsive interface around quick capture.
- Built and tested a clickable prototype with early users.

## A little more

The best tools leave room for the person using them. I kept the visual language warm and deliberately quiet, so the ideas—not the interface—could take center stage.
